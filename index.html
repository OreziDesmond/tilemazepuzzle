Game Pitch

Meet Tile Maze Puzzle

A physics-based puzzle game built around simple controls, spatial decision-making and reactive tile mechanics. Players guide balls through increasingly challenging maze layouts, manipulating the environment and managing momentum to get enough balls safely to the finish.

At a Glance

  • Genre: Physics Puzzle / Casual
  • Core Loop: Spawn • Guide • Adapt • Complete
  • Controls: Simple, touch-first interaction
  • Progression: Level-based challenge escalation
  • Platform: Mobile
  • Status: Playable build available for evaluation
Why it can workCore Game Proposition

Immediately Understandable

The objective is clear from the first interaction: control the balls, navigate the maze and get enough of them to the finish.

Physics Creates Variety

Momentum, positioning and level geometry make each challenge feel interactive rather than purely pattern-based.

Room to Scale

The level-based structure supports progressively more complex layouts, mechanics and challenge combinations.
